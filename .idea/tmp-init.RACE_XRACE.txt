 ** Costs and tech requirements are preset, do not modify
 ** Each tech type is grouped cleanly (Presences, Memes, Focuses, Monorace, and Synergy)
 ** Each tech type has a specific number of positive and negative values and preset intervals; no need to adjust these
 ** Some tech types have ranks; the effect MULs will scale with rank; no need to adjust these
 ** Simply replace each XBOOST one-by-one with a real boost key
 
 ** DESIGN PHILOSHOPHY: Effects are chosen based on tech type
  ** PRESENCES x3: Gives preset Gov points with 1 small flavor-only effect (+/-10%; 0 ranks)
  ** FOCUSES x2: Works as a specialization of a race; affects ONLY this race. 3 positives, 2 negatives (+/-10% per rank; 3 ranks)
  ** MEMES x4: Major city-wide traits that affect ALL races; each should be impactful. 3 positives, 1 negative (+/-30%, 0 ranks)  
  ** MONORACE x1: Massive playthrough-defining effects; requires non-slaves are ONLY this race. Allows niche playstyle otherwise not possible. 1 major positives, 1 major negative (+/-15% per rank); 3 positives, 2 negatives (+/-10% per rank); 6 ranks 
  ** SYNERGY x1: Rewards specific race-combo; the thematic opposite of Monorace. Same preset values as Monorace.
